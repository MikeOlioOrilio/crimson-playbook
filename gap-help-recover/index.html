Gap help: ball, you, man

Pressure the ball while weak-side defenders shift toward the center and keep sight of their own players.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

5-on-5 shell, half court. Xn starts guarding n. Begin with pass-only offense, then allow a single drive.

Three coaching cues

  1. On ball: contain first; hands active without touching.
  2. One pass away: shrink the gap without losing the closeout.
  3. Two passes away: see ball and man near the help line; do not camp blindly in the paint.

Run it at practice

9 minutes • Freeze on each pass and ask each player to name ball and matchup. Progress pass-only → one drive → live. Defense earns a point only for a stop plus rebound.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

Wing catch • Shift together: Ball moves to 2 on the right. X2 closes; X1 takes the gap; weak-side X3 and X4 shift inward while tracking their players.

Middle drive • Help and recover: 2 beats X2 toward the middle. X1 briefly stops the drive; when 2 passes back to 1, X1 recovers on the pass.
