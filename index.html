A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Do we need technical experience?+

Employee workshops can start with the work you already understand. Technical experience becomes more relevant for data connections and system integrations. We match the starting point to your team.

Is this just about writing better prompts?+

Clear instructions help, but the bigger skill is directing a complete piece of work: supplying context, choosing tools, breaking down a goal, refining the output, and checking whether it is useful and correct.

Do spreadsheets still have a place?+

Yes—when they suit the job. AI can help analyze a workbook, explain calculations, or explore data. It can also help you build a better tool or workflow when a spreadsheet has become a bottleneck. The task determines the approach.

Will AI get everything right?+

No. It can produce convincing mistakes and miss important context. Training includes verification, source checking, testing, and recognizing when a person with the right expertise needs to make the call.

Can it work with our documents and software?+

Often, depending on your tools, licensing, permissions, and data quality. Connected AI retrieves approved information; integrated AI can take defined actions. Both need deliberate setup and testing. Not every system supports the same capabilities.
