FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which level should my organization start with?

It depends on your goals and readiness. Many organizations start with Level 1 for fast, organization-wide productivity gains, or Level 2 when they have a specific knowledge-heavy use case such as sales or support.

Can we adopt more than one level at a time?

Absolutely. The levels complement each other. For example, employees may use AI tools daily (Level 1) that also draw on company knowledge (Level 2), while automated workflows (Level 3) run in the background.

Do we need to buy new software?

Often, no. Many of the biggest Level 1 gains come from AI features and automation already built into the software you own. When new tools are needed, we recommend options that fit your budget and environment.

Is our company data safe?

Yes. We prioritize enterprise-grade tools with strong privacy commitments, apply permission-aware access to your data, and help you establish clear AI usage policies so sensitive information stays protected.

How quickly can we see results?

Level 1 rollouts can show productivity gains within a few weeks. Level 2 pilots typically take 4–8 weeks, and Level 3 integrations vary based on system complexity. We always start with a focused pilot to demonstrate value quickly.
