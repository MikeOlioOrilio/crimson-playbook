    The first thing every learner should hear

    02 / CHOOSE THE RIGHT SURFACE

    Three platforms. Different strengths.

    Desktop is the starting point when a lesson needs local files or apps. For chat, research, or a cloud task, the web or phone may be simpler. Check the actual device, account, and permissions before promising a feature.

    03 / PRACTICE THE RESTORE

    Know your way back.

    Ask the assistant for a recovery plan before it edits anything. Make and verify a backup yourself. A promise in a chat, a synced folder, or a provider’s Undo button is not proof of a recoverable copy.
