Baseline stack: burst to space

A staggered burst creates distinct passing windows without asking smaller players to win jump balls.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

3 inbounding; four players in a loose angled stack outside the lane. These are cuts, not moving screens.

Three coaching cues

  1. Leave at different beats, not all at once.
  2. First cutter goes toward the rim; next goes wide.
  3. Last player becomes safety outside the lane. Never push through defenders.

Run it at practice

6 minutes • Walk the burst, then defenders deny different windows. Rotate inbounders each rep. Success is a clean catch within five seconds with no two cutters in the same lane.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

Rim defender late • First cut: X5 is slow to follow the first cut. 5 changes speed into the open rim window.

Rim covered • Safety: X5 tracks 5 and X2 takes the wide cut. 4 breaks back high while X4 sags; use the safety entry.

Inbound cue: identify the safety early and release within five seconds of the ball being at the thrower’s disposal. Request a timeout before the violation only if one is available. On a designated-spot throw-in, stay within the spot; endline movement after a made basket is different.
