Early drag: turn the corner

One early ball screen in transition, with a simple read of the screener’s defender.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

Flow from the break into four-out spacing; 5 arrives as the trailer. Any player can screen.

Three coaching cues

  1. 1 waits until 5 is planted.
  2. Use the screen close to the hip without steering into contact.
  3. 5 rolls only after 1 clears; skip a crowded pocket pass and reverse.

Run it at practice

7 minutes • Begin 2-on-2 with a passer in the corner. X5 alternates staying back and stepping up. Add weak-side help only when the first two reads are reliable.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

X5 steps up • Pocket to 5: X5 leaves the roll to contain 1. X1 trails over the screen; 5 rolls into the pocket.

X5 stays on roll • Keep it: X5 retreats with 5 and X1 trails. 1 keeps the ball into the open lane; finish only if help stays home.

Screen cue: arrive, plant, wait, cut, release. Stay stationary with arms close before contact. Give an unseen stationary defender a normal step and a moving defender time and distance to avoid the screen. The gold bar marks the screening side; do not chase a defender with a moving screen.
