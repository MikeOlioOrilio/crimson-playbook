FUNDAMENTALS / Closeouts & shot defense

Make it hard. Keep it clean.

Mustang defends Thunder: arrive under control, contest in your own space, and protect the landing area.

Your job

The goal is a difficult shot without unnecessary contact. No pose guarantees a no-call: timing, legal guarding position, and the actual contact matter. These are three teaching snapshots; the final one is a separate close-range verticality example.

PHOTO WALKTHROUGH

Clear stills · one action at a time

3 photo steps

STEP 01

Brake before the shooter

Arrive fast enough to help, slow enough to stop.

  • Close the distance, then shorten your steps and lower your hips before reaching the attacker. Keep enough room to respond to a drive.
  • Raise a hand without putting it on the player. Do not sprint through the shooter or fly past a shot fake.
Catch this mistake

Closing so fast that you cannot stop without bumping the shooter.

STEP 02

Contest with space

Hand toward the release. Body under control.

  • For this perimeter teaching rep, stay grounded and make the release harder with a high hand. Keep that hand away from the shooter’s wrist and arm.
  • Leave the shooter room to land. If you are late, avoid launching into their path just to try for a block.
Catch this mistake

Swatting down, touching the shooting arm, or putting a foot underneath the shooter.

STEP 03

Go vertical at the rim

Establish position, then stay in your own space.

  • This is a different situation near the basket. Establish legal guarding position before the opponent is airborne, then raise your arms within your vertical plane.
  • You may jump straight up from legal position. Do not lean, drift forward, or lower the arms onto the attacker. Finish the possession by finding the rebound.
Catch this mistake

Thinking hands-up makes any body contact legal. Late position and forward movement still matter.

KEEP IT LEGAL

Know the boundary

Keep the shooter’s airborne and landing space clear. The official judges the complete play, not just whether the defender’s hands were up.

TRY IT AT PRACTICE

Start slow, then add a read

Partner catches, sometimes shoots and sometimes shot-fakes. Defender closes, brakes, and contests without touching. Start at half speed. Separately rehearse vertical arms at the rim without body contact; add live play only after control is consistent.

Ask yourself

Can I contest and still stop in my own space?
