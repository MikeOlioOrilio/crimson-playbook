YOUR QUESTIONS, WELCOME

A new skill.
A comfortable start.

Is this for me if my employer isn’t involved?+

Yes. Individual training is for your own goals, whether or not AI is part of your job. You can invest in your learning without an employer arranging or paying for it.

Do I need to know anything about AI?+

No. We can start with what AI can do, how to use it, and how to ask useful questions. If you already use it, we can focus on more involved projects and more effective ways of working.

What can we work on together?+

A personal project, a subject you want to learn, career preparation, creative ideas, research, or everyday organization. The examples above are starting points. Your goal guides the session.

Is this one-to-one or a class?+

The individual offering includes one-to-one training and small-group workshops. One-to-one sessions focus on your questions and pace; group sessions combine guided examples with time to practice.

Do I need a paid AI subscription?+

That depends on your goals and the tools you choose. Some exercises can use free tools; other features require a paid plan. We can identify what’s needed before choosing a learning approach.

Will AI do the thinking for me?+

The aim is to expand what you can do while building your own understanding. You’ll practice explaining your goal, questioning the output, and checking the result. AI can make mistakes; your judgment remains essential.
