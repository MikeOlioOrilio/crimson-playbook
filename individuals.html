YOUR QUESTIONS, WELCOME

A new skill.
A comfortable start.

Are all the courses about AI?+

No. Personal Finance Essentials and Personal Scam Prevention focus on everyday knowledge and decisions. Personal Safe AI Use covers responsible use of AI. Separate AI practice courses provide files and guided exercises to try with your own assistant.

Can I enroll without an employer?+

Yes. You can choose Individual Focus or Professional Focus with the same account, whether you enroll on your own or also have company-sponsored training.

Do I need to know anything about AI?+

No AI experience is needed for personal finance or scam-awareness courses. If you want to start using AI, the beginner course helps you choose a platform and practice your first requests. You can then move into guided projects at your own pace.

Can someone help me set up AI in person?+

Yes. In Washington County, Utah, request help setting up AI on the phone, tablet, or computer you already use. See the hourly rates and request a session.

Do I need a paid AI subscription?+

Certifications and their assessments run in Real Knowledge without a separate ChatGPT subscription or personal API key. For AI practice courses, use your chosen outside assistant. Many activities work with a free plan; some features require a separately purchased paid plan.

Will AI do the thinking for me?+

The aim of AI practice is to help you direct a task and check the result. You’ll explain your goal, question the output, and compare it with the source. AI can make mistakes; your judgment remains essential.

Where do I return to my learning or certificates?+

Open My Dashboard to continue your courses, retrieve certificates, and check the next review date. Sign in with the Real Knowledge account you used to enroll.
