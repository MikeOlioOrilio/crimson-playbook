REAL KNOWLEDGE LLC

Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Real Knowledge LLC operates realknowledgeonline.com and learn.realknowledgeonline.com. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our websites, contact us, use learning and certification services, manage company training or documents, or choose to share a certificate.

For privacy questions or requests, email info@realknowledgeonline.com.

1. Information we handle

  • Accounts and profiles. Email address, account identifier, sign-in and email-verification information, and the first and last name you provide for your profile or certificate. Firebase Authentication handles account credentials.
  • Learning and certificates. Course access, assignments, sections reviewed, assessment answers, AI-generated grading feedback, results, scores, completion dates, certificate names and IDs, issue and expiration dates, renewal activity, and email-delivery or reminder preferences.
  • Company services. Company and contact details, logos, owner and manager information, employee email rosters, assigned products, enrollment and completion records, uploaded documents and extracted text, generated training drafts, document-sharing choices, and related billing records.
  • Questions and practice. Text you submit to prompt coaching, platform guidance, document Q&A, or other AI-assisted features, together with relevant selections and the response needed to provide that feature.
  • Messages and requests. Your name, reply email, message, and any optional phone number, company, city, or requested session details you send through our contact forms or email.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. Product, amount, payment status, billing contact, transaction references, and subscription or renewal status. When checkout is available, Stripe collects payment details through its checkout or billing pages; our application does not collect full card numbers or card security codes.
  • Technical and operational information. Sign-in, request, error, delivery, usage, and security information. Our hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, browser information, and request metadata. We also keep aggregate operational counts, such as contact inquiries and AI-feature usage.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide purchased or company-assigned access, save progress, assess answers, issue and verify certificates, manage company documents and assignments, process billing, respond to requests, and maintain the services.

We send service messages such as verification emails, certificates, assignment notices, billing messages, and certificate-expiration reminders. Company accounts may enable completion notices or summary reports. Authorized Real Knowledge personnel may access records when needed for administration, support, maintenance, security, or resolving a request.

3. Company accounts and private documents

If a company enrolls you, it supplies information such as your email address and assignments. Authorized company owners, managers, and administrators can see the roster, assigned products, access, and completion information made available by their account. Enabled reports may send learner emails and completion or outstanding-assignment details to the company's selected notification contact.

Documents used for Custom Company Certifications and Company Knowledge Q&A are restricted through company account and sharing permissions. They can include manuals, safety information, project records, procedures, policies, and other company references. Authorized company users can manage these materials, and assigned or permitted learners can access the relevant learning or documents. Source documents, assessment answers, and scores are not displayed by public certificate confirmation. If a learner issues a named company certification record, its ID can confirm the holder's name and whether it is valid or expired.

Company subscription administration records include employee access assignments, certification-seat use within each paid billing period, access removals and acknowledgments, and exchanges of previously used seats. Authorized company managers and Real Knowledge administrators can review the records available to their roles.

Processing a private document may involve our storage, hosting, and AI providers as described below. Upload only material you are authorized to use, and avoid unnecessary personal records, credentials, or sensitive information. Contact your company administrator about company-controlled assignments, document sharing, or the company's own handling of employee information.

Optional Google Drive imports. You authorize Google access and choose a file through Google Drive Picker. This feature uses per-file access rather than access to your entire Drive. Google Docs are converted to a PDF snapshot; other supported files are imported as copies. When you submit the document for upload, the copy is saved in private company storage. Imports do not sync automatically: replace or re-import the document to bring in later changes.

Our app keeps Google OAuth access tokens only in browser memory during the import; it does not send them to or store them on our backend. Imported copies and extracted text follow the company access, AI processing, retention, replacement, and deletion practices described in this policy.

You can revoke the app's Google access in your Google Account connections settings. Disconnecting Google does not remove copies already imported. Remove the document through your company account or contact us using the deletion-request options below.

4. AI-assisted features

We use OpenAI's API for features such as written-answer grading, prompt coaching, platform guidance, preparing company-document training, document previews, and document Q&A. Depending on the feature, requests can include your submitted text, written answers, relevant course material, company-document text or selected excerpts, and instructions needed to produce a response.

The public website's “Chat with Us” guide is an AI assistant, not a live representative. Your question and a short recent conversation are sent to OpenAI to help explain our public offerings. Chat history stays in the current page's memory until you clear it, leave, or reload the page. Our application does not save the raw chat transcript; it keeps limited hashed usage counters to help prevent abuse. Hosting and AI provider processing and retention can still apply. Do not submit passwords, payment details, or private company documents.

Our current Responses API requests disable OpenAI's stored-response option. Provider processing for safety, security, abuse monitoring, and legal obligations can still apply; this setting does not mean that every copy is immediately deleted. See OpenAI's API data controls for its retention and processing details.

If a practice course asks you to use your own outside AI account, information you submit there is handled under that platform's terms and privacy settings.

5. Certificate verification and sharing

Anyone with a certificate ID or its verification link can use our public confirmation tool. For a matching, eligible certificate, it displays the certificate holder's name and whether the certificate is valid or expired. The public confirmation result does not display the learner's email, assessment answers, or score. Standard certificate confirmation can depend on the underlying access record. An issued company certification retains its recorded expiration date when a company subscription ends; company renewal reminders require active assigned access. Deleting certificate records can prevent future confirmation.

A certificate image or PDF contains the details printed on it, including your name, certificate title, ID, and relevant dates. When you download, email, or publish that file or its verification link, the people who receive it can retain or share it.

6. Optional LinkedIn sharing

Where offered, sharing a certificate to LinkedIn is optional. You connect your LinkedIn account, review the certificate image, message, and verification link, and explicitly approve the post. Earning a certificate or connecting LinkedIn does not automatically publish a post.

For that sharing session, we receive the LinkedIn member identifier and authorization needed to publish the post you approve. We temporarily store the member access token on our server with a 15-minute session limit. Expired sessions are not accepted for posting, and stored connection data is removed through cleanup. A temporary copy can remain until that cleanup runs.

The approved image, message, and link are sent to LinkedIn and become visible according to the post's audience and LinkedIn's rules. Removing our temporary connection data does not delete an existing LinkedIn post or necessarily revoke a token already issued by LinkedIn. Manage or delete published posts on LinkedIn, and remove the app's access in LinkedIn's Permitted Services settings. You can also email info@realknowledgeonline.com to request removal of connection data we still hold. See LinkedIn's Privacy Policy.

7. Service providers

We use providers to operate the services, including:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud for hosting, authentication, database records, file storage, server processing, and operational logs. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Google email services to send and receive contact, account, certificate, company, and billing messages; Google reCAPTCHA to help protect contact forms from spam; and Google Fonts to load website typefaces. These services can receive information needed to deliver their features. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI for the AI processing described above, and Stripe for enabled payment and subscription services. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • LinkedIn when you choose the sharing feature described above.

We may disclose information when needed to respond to legal requirements, protect accounts and services, investigate misuse, or protect people's rights and safety.

8. Browser storage and technical information

Our sites and sign-in services use browser storage and related technologies to maintain authentication and service state. Some training answers or practice progress are saved in local storage on your device; contact-return notices use session storage. The marketing site checks sign-in status with the learner site so it can show the appropriate account controls.

You can clear site data or change browser storage settings, though doing so can sign you out, remove locally saved progress, or prevent features from working. Hosting, authentication, reCAPTCHA, fonts, and other provider requests also involve technical information described in their privacy policies.

9. Retention and deletion

We retain account, access, learning, certificate, company, billing, and communication records as needed to provide the services and handle support, security, disputes, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. We do not apply one fixed retention period to every type of information. Certificate expiration alone does not delete its records.

Standard certification progress keeps the current saved answers and results and up to the five most recent submitted assessment rounds. Replacing or deleting company documents and publishing updated private training triggers removal of superseded material and related records according to that feature's workflow. Cleanup can take time and may retry if a provider is unavailable.

The signed-in certification records page lets you stop expiration reminders or delete standard certification records. That deletion removes issued standard certificates, their course-progress records, and reminder links; it does not by itself delete your sign-in account, saved profile, purchase or access records, company-managed records, all emails, or every record held by a provider. A minimal stopped-reminders preference remains.

For broader account or data deletion, email info@realknowledgeonline.com. We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with a company account owner. Some billing, legal, security, or dispute records may need to be retained. Information already delivered to email recipients, downloaded by others, or posted to LinkedIn is also subject to those recipients' or providers' controls.

10. Your choices and requests

You can update the name in your learner profile, manage supported billing or subscription options, stop certificate-expiration reminders, and choose whether to share a certificate. Stopping reminders does not cancel a subscription, and deleting certification records does not cancel billing.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold, or to ask about a privacy choice, contact info@realknowledgeonline.com from the email associated with your account when possible. Describe the request without sending your password, full payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information. We will explain any verification needed and any limitations that apply.

11. Security and policy updates

We use account authentication, access controls, private storage permissions, and other measures to protect information. No online service or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

We may update this policy as the services or our practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Privacy questions can be sent to Real Knowledge LLC at info@realknowledgeonline.com.
