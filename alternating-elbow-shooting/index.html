Alternating elbows: shoot, rebound, switch

Two lines of five, three balls, and six alternating shots. Rebound your own shot, exit toward the opposite sideline, feed the waiting player, and follow the outside edge to the back.

Set it up

10 players, 1 basket, 3 balls. Lines start at the free-throw-line elbows and extend diagonally back toward the sidelines at about 45°. L1 and R1 each have a ball; L2 holds the third. Labels show the starting group and stay with each player.

Three coaching cues

  1. The next shooter releases only when the previous shooter controls the rebound. Waiting players step up after the elbow clears.
  2. On the rebound, immediately head out toward the opposite sideline. Pass from outside the lane to the next unfilled player in that line.
  3. After the pass, continue along that sideline and around the back of the line. Do not return through the middle or between waiting players.

Run it at practice

30-second demonstration • Shot order: L1, R1, L2, R2, L3, R3. In practice, keep the rotation going and count makes. Adjust the distance to preserve balanced form. Three balls keep one ready shooter supplied while a rebounder passes to the player behind. First walk the exchange, then use a steady shooting rhythm.

The sample ends after three shots per side. At 26s, L4 is ready to begin the next live cycle, but that seventh shot is not shown. R3 finishes by feeding L5 behind L4 and joining the left line. Continuous loop replays this example from its original setup; it does not extend the live rotation.

Drill sequence

30 seconds • Six alternating shots: L1 → R1 → L2 → R2 → L3 → R3. Each rebound cues the opposite shooter; the rebounder exits to the opposite sideline, feeds the waiting player, and follows that sideline to the back. Three balls: A, B and C.
