Made basket: get it in & go

Two changing entry windows start the break without a high, risky pass over taller defenders.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

Full court after an opponent’s made basket. 3 starts outside the left baseline; 1/2 show short, 4 is middle, 5 stretches deep.

Three coaching cues

  1. Receivers change pace and come to the pass.
  2. 3 may move along the endline after a made basket.
  3. On a designated-spot throw-in, stay within that spot instead.

Run it at practice

7 minutes • Defenders deny one receiver, then choose freely. Switch inbounder after each trip. Goal: release by the count of four, then reach the frontcourt under control.

The five-second throw-in count starts when the ball is at the thrower’s disposal. Here the release occurs early; the remaining animation shows the next pass.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

1 denied • Find 2: X1 denies 1. 2 cuts back toward the inbounder away from X2; 3 moves along the endline to improve the angle.

2 denied • Find 1: X2 denies 2. 1 returns toward the baseline to create separation and receives the short entry.

Inbound cue: identify the safety early and release within five seconds of the ball being at the thrower’s disposal. Request a timeout before the violation only if one is available. On a designated-spot throw-in, stay within the spot; endline movement after a made basket is different.
