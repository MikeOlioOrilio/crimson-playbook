Screen lab: curl or slip

Read the screener’s defender. A planted screen can free the cutter—or the screener.

Choose what the defense does

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

3 attackers, 2 guided defenders and a coach watching screen legality. Add the passer’s defender for live 3-on-3.

Three coaching cues

  1. 5 arrives on X2’s intended chase path and plants.
  2. 2 waits, then cuts close to 5 without hitting a teammate.
  3. 5 stays set until 2 clears; show hands after releasing.

Run it at practice

8 minutes • 3 guided reps of each read, then live 3-on-3. Rotate all roles after 3 reps. A legal screen plus correct read earns a point, even on a missed finish.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

X5 follows 2 • Pass 5: X5 takes the cutter 2. X2 is delayed by the screen, leaving 5 open after the cutter clears.

X5 stays with 5 • Pass 2: X5 stays attached to 5. X2 trails the screen, so 2 curls into the open rim lane.

Screen cue: arrive, plant, wait, cut, release. Stay stationary with arms close before contact. Give an unseen stationary defender a normal step and a moving defender time and distance to avoid the screen. The gold bar marks the screening side; do not chase a defender with a moving screen.
