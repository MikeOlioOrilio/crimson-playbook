Dribble-at: backdoor

Punish tight wing denial with a cut behind the defender instead of a risky pass over the top.

Choose what the defense does

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

Start five-out. 1 dribbles toward 2; 5 lifts out of the ball-side corner to clear the cut.

Three coaching cues

  1. Eye contact before the dribble-at.
  2. 2 sells a step toward the ball, then plants and cuts behind denial.
  3. If the defender sinks, stay spaced and make the safe pass.

Run it at practice

6 minutes • Rehearse 2-on-2 plus a corner spacer, then 5-on-5. Defender chooses denial or cushion. Score one coaching point for correctly declining a covered backdoor.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

X2 denies • Backdoor: X2 steps above 2 to deny the wing. 2 goes behind; 5 lifts to clear the baseline lane.

X2 sinks • Simple entry: X2 drops toward the rim. Do not cut into the defender; 2 stays wide for a normal wing catch.
