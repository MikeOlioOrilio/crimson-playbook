3-on-3: pass, cut, read

Give your guards more live touches and a reason to move after every pass.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

6 players, half court, 1 ball. Start with a top and two wings; no dribbles for the first round.

Three coaching cues

  1. Pass, then cut with eyes on the ball.
  2. Weak wing fills the empty top spot after the cut clears.
  3. A covered cut is useful: clear the lane and keep playing.

Run it at practice

8 minutes • Games to 3 stops or scores, rotate after each game. Start with one guided read, then play freely. Add one dribble per catch once passes and spacing are sound.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

X1 watches ball • Hit cutter: X1 turns toward 2 and loses the cut. 2 passes into the open lane to 1.

X1 follows • Reverse: X1 follows 1 into the lane. 2 reverses to 3 at the top and 1 fills the empty wing.
