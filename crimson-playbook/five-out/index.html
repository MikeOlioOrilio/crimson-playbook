Five-out: pass, cut, fill

Your base offense: open the paint and let all five players pass, cut, and attack.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

Five spots outside the lane: top, two wings, two corners. Space to passing ability; nobody is assigned to stand under the rim.

Three coaching cues

  1. Look at the rim on every catch.
  2. Cut with purpose; if covered, clear all the way to the open corner.
  3. Fill behind a cutter, never into the same space.

Run it at practice

9 minutes • Walk 5-on-0, play 3-on-3, then 5-on-5. First target: five possessions with a cut and correct fill, regardless of scoring. Add screens only after this is comfortable.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

X1 late • Give-and-go: X1 watches the pass and stays high. 1 cuts into space and 2 delivers the return.

X1 follows • Fill and reverse: X1 tracks the cutter. 1 clears left and the ball reverses to 3 at the top.
