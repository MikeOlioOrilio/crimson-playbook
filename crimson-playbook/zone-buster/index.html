Zone buster: nail & short corner

Keep this as an answer to an opponent’s 2–3 zone. A quick decision at the foul line replaces a size mismatch.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

5 attackers against a guided 2–3 zone. 4 is any confident catch-and-pass player; 5 works the short corner.

Three coaching cues

  1. 4 catches at the nail facing the rim.
  2. 5 moves behind the middle defender; do not park in the lane.
  3. Corner spacing stays within each player’s useful shooting or driving range.

Run it at practice

7 minutes • Guide the middle defender, then add the low defender’s choice. Rotate every player through the nail. Target an advantage in two passes after the nail catch.

This page teaches offense against a zone. All five defensive strategies in this playbook use man-to-man responsibilities.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

Middle steps up • Find 5: X5 leaves the rim to close 4. X4 stays with 2; 5 cuts behind X5 into the high-low window.

Low defender tags • Find 2: X4 sinks to take 5 after X5 steps to 4. Pass to 2; the catch creates a shot or closeout drive, not a guaranteed three.
