Ball screen: talk, contain, recover

One base coverage: the guard works over and the screener’s defender contains until the guard recovers.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

5-on-5 or 2-on-2 plus a weak-side helper. Begin with a slow ball screen; X5 calls the side early.

Three coaching cues

  1. X1 gets over the screen without holding or shoving.
  2. X5 contains at a depth that prevents a free drive and a free roll.
  3. X4 briefly tags a dangerous roller, then recovers on the pass.

Run it at practice

8 minutes • Four guided reps each side, then live 3-on-3. Judge containment, recovery, and rebounding. Agree any switching call separately; do not mix coverages silently.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

Ball handler keeps • Recover: X5 contains 1 for a moment while X1 works over. X1 returns to the ball and X5 takes 5 again.

Pass to roller • Tag and recover: 1 passes to 5 before X5 gets back. Weak-side X4 tags the roller; X5 recovers and X4 gets home.

Screen cue: arrive, plant, wait, cut, release. Stay stationary with arms close before contact. Give an unseen stationary defender a normal step and a moving defender time and distance to avoid the screen. The gold bar marks the screening side; do not chase a defender with a moving screen.
