Full-court man: turn & contain

Extend your man defense to use speed without abandoning the rim or gambling for steals.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

5-on-5, full court. Assign matchups before the inbound. Start pressure after the catch; no automatic trap.

Three coaching cues

  1. Nearest defender contains the ball; others stay basket-side of their players.
  2. Influence a sideline without opening a straight drive to the rim.
  3. If beaten, sprint to recover and protect the basket before hunting the ball.

Run it at practice

8 minutes • Play from inbound to a half-court advantage, then reset. Score defense for delaying a clean advance without fouling. Add live possessions after all five can identify their matchup.

Use only when your event permits pressing. Confirm grade restrictions and any lead-based no-press rule with the organizer.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

Dribbler advances • Turn: X1 stays in front and angles 1 away from a straight middle drive. Others advance with their assignments.

Pass beats pressure • Recover: 1 passes ahead to 3. X3 contains the new ball handler while X1 recovers to 1; nobody chases the ball blindly.
