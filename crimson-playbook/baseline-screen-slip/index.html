Baseline: screen & slip

One screen, two reads, and a visible safety—an easy first inbound to install.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

3 inbounding on the right baseline. 2 at the right block, 5 inside the elbow, 1 high safety, 4 weak wing.

Three coaching cues

  1. 5 sets on the rim side of X2.
  2. 2 cuts across the screen; 5 holds until the cutter clears.
  3. 3 reads X5; 1 stays available if both options close.

Run it at practice

7 minutes • 3 guided reps per coverage, then live. Switch inbound sides. Aim for 4 of 5 clean entries released by the count of four.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

X5 stays with 5 • Find 2: X5 stays near screener 5. X2 trails the screen and 2 cuts into the rim window.

X5 follows 2 • Find 5: X5 follows cutter 2. Once 2 clears, 5 releases toward the ball into the space X5 left.

Screen cue: arrive, plant, wait, cut, release. Stay stationary with arms close before contact. Give an unseen stationary defender a normal step and a moving defender time and distance to avoid the screen. The gold bar marks the screening side; do not chase a defender with a moving screen.
Inbound cue: identify the safety early and release within five seconds of the ball being at the thrower’s disposal. Request a timeout before the violation only if one is available. On a designated-spot throw-in, stay within the spot; endline movement after a made basket is different.
