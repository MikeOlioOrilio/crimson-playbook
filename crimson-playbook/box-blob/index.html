Box: screen the screener

Your original box action, rebuilt with two planted screens and a real safety read.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

5-on-5. 3 throws in from the baseline; 1/2 at elbows, 4/5 at blocks. Numbers are jobs, not height requirements.

Three coaching cues

  1. 1 sets below X4; 4 cuts only after the plant.
  2. 4 sets on the rim side of X5; 5 brushes the open hip.
  3. 3 scans the rim, then safety. Get the ball released within five seconds.

Run it at practice

8 minutes • Walk both screens, then give defenders one coverage to show. Alternate inbound sides. Success = legal screens and 4 of 5 clean entries, not only layups.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

X1 stays • Find 5: X1 follows 1 out to the safety spot while X5 is delayed. 5 cuts behind the planted screen into the baseline passing window.

X1 helps on 5 • Find 1: X1 leaves 1 to cover 5 at the basket. 1 has popped outside with space; pass to 1, then flow into offense.

Screen cue: arrive, plant, wait, cut, release. Stay stationary with arms close before contact. Give an unseen stationary defender a normal step and a moving defender time and distance to avoid the screen. The gold bar marks the screening side; do not chase a defender with a moving screen.
Inbound cue: identify the safety early and release within five seconds of the ball being at the thrower’s disposal. Request a timeout before the violation only if one is available. On a designated-spot throw-in, stay within the spot; endline movement after a made basket is different.
