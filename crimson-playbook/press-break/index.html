Press break: middle, back, opposite

Give the ball handler three short options. Advance by passing and spacing instead of racing into a trap.

Step 1

Set up

Pause at the decision. Ask: “What did the defender do? Where is the space?”

Set it up

5-on-5, full court. Start after a made-basket entry. 3 is middle, 4 is reversal, 5 stretches beyond the pressure.

Three coaching cues

  1. Catch facing the court and protect the ball.
  2. Keep one teammate behind and one in the middle.
  3. After crossing midcourt, do not reverse into the backcourt.

Run it at practice

8 minutes • Guided traps, then live pressure. Require a middle or reversal touch before attacking. Rotate the middle role. Goal: 4 of 5 advances without a turnover; teach the ten-second backcourt count.

Walk through the read

All defensive reads

Middle open • Turn and go: X3 overplays toward 5. 3 flashes behind the first line and catches in the middle.

Middle denied • Reverse: X3 stays on 3 and the two front defenders close toward 1. Retreat and reverse to 4, then attack the opposite side.
