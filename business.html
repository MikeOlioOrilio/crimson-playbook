A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Is everything here about AI?+

No. Professional certifications also cover business ethics, payment-card awareness, information protection, and everyday security. Company Knowledge Q&A and Custom Company Certifications use the information relevant to your business. AI practice courses and consulting are separate options.

Do employees need their own AI accounts?+

Certifications and Company Knowledge Q&A run in Real Knowledge with the required learner access. AI practice courses use the outside platform your company chooses; any required AI license is purchased separately.

Do we need technical experience?+

Online learning and company document tools do not require employees to be programmers. AI workshops can start with work your team already understands. Custom data connections and integrations need technical scoping, suitable tools, and testing.

Is AI training just about writing better prompts?+

Clear instructions help, but the bigger skill is directing a complete task: supplying context, choosing tools, refining the output, and checking whether it is useful and correct. Practice courses include realistic files to work with.

Which company information can we use?+

Use documentation about your tools, products, safety practices, projects, events, processes, or policies. Company Knowledge Q&A helps employees ask about an approved document they select. Custom Company Certifications let you focus learning and assessments on the information team members need to understand.

Will AI get everything right?+

No. It can produce convincing mistakes and miss important context. Company owners should review generated learning and assessments before publishing. Employees can check Q&A answers against the quoted document sections. Important or safety-related decisions still need the current source, company procedures, and appropriate review.

Can it work with our documents and software?+

Company Knowledge Q&A answers questions from the approved documents you share with authorized users. Connections to other software or workflows depend on licensing, permissions, APIs, and data quality, and may need a separately scoped project. Not every system supports the same capabilities.

How do we get started?+
